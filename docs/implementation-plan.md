# Implementation plan

What to build next, in order. Design and reasons: [overview.md](overview.md). Source: [spec.md](spec.md). Each milestone is agreed with Jonathan before it starts; new dependencies (for example `opentimelineio`, the MCP Python SDK) need his decision first.

## R1: a tape in, a highlight reel out (family VHS)

Goal: one digitised VHS tape becomes a list of scenes a person can browse and an automatic highlight reel of a chosen length, which they can correct and render. Everything after ingest runs as queued, resumable jobs.

1. **Sources and derived assets, from the raw footage.** *(Done 2026-11-23: sources with `app add` and `/api/sources`; the `hash` job; the `proxy`, `thumbnails` and `peaks` jobs in `derived.py` on strata-python's `derive`.)* Add a tape: fingerprint, `media.probe`, store as an immutable source; a background job computes its full SHA-256 (the fingerprint samples only the ends), then queues its derived assets: a browser proxy (deinterlaced for display, square pixels, a keyframe every half second, the source's frames at the source's times), thumbnails every 10 s and audio peaks. Each is a result with a file (`derived/<cache key>/` in the app's home), keyed on the source's hash, the recipe and its version. Ingest works on the raw tape only: restoration is step 7 (Jonathan, 2026-11-23).
1b. *(Done 2026-11-23: `results.py`, `overrides.py`, `jobs.py`, `app jobs`, `app work`.)* **The records every later step writes into** (overview §6), before step 2 stores a result: the result, override and job tables with their provenance, and the job runner on `strata_python.tasks.queue`. Needs strata-models to report, for each job, the model and a hash of its settings, and the client to return them with the result (strata-models #103, PR #104: `run_with_provenance` returns `{model, options, settings_hash, job_id}`, which `results.Provenance.of` takes as it comes).
2. **Scenes.** *(Started 2026-11-24: the `shots`, `keyframes` and `scenes` jobs, `app scenes`; shots from strata-models' ffmpeg baseline, scenes grouped by CLIP similarity of each shot's middle frame and black between shots, with every cut kept as a boundary with its confidence and reasons. Still to do: the `shots-spliced` run on real race footage and a check on a real tape, both on the GPU machine; the grouping's thresholds measured against scenes a person marks.)* Split the tape into shots and group them into scenes. Needs a shot-boundary role in strata-models with an eval; ffmpeg's scene score is the baseline to beat (served; its eval `shots-spliced` scores it on uncut race files spliced at known points, including jumps within one file, the hard cut). Before the role is trusted on tapes, run `shots-spliced` on the real race footage on the GPU machine and add a small check on tape itself: the spliced clips are clean and progressive, so VHS faults (tracking noise, the glitch at a record-pause cut, head-switching at the bottom of the frame) are not measured yet. Each scene keeps its boundary confidence (spec S5).
3. **Per-scene analysis.** *(Started 2026-11-24: the `transcript`, `faces` and `describe` jobs; `app scenes` shows each scene's summary and face count. A transcript of the whole tape from `transcribe` with word timings, Whisper's doubtful segments marked; a description of each scene from strata-models' new role `scene-describe` (preset `scene-describe-v1`, strata-models #120) on up to three of its keyframes and its speech; faces on every keyframe, boxes only. Still to do: a run on a real tape on the GPU machine and an eval of the describer (Qwen3.5-9B against Claude Haiku) on tape scenes; the thumbnail strip is step 4's screen, from the thumbnails step 1 makes.)* A thumbnail strip; a transcript of any speech (`transcribe`); a short description of what is happening (`vision-language` on sampled frames, observed facts kept apart from guesses, spec S4); the faces present (`faces`). Every result records its role, model and inputs.
4. **The tape's chapters.** A screen listing the scenes with thumbnails, descriptions and length; play any scene; correct a boundary or a description (stored as an override, not an edit of the model's output).
5. **The highlight proposal.** Given a target length, choose and trim scenes into a reel. A deterministic baseline first (scores from description, faces, speech and picture quality), the language-model planner after. The proposal is a pure function of (analysis, overrides, seed); locks, pins and bans survive re-planning.
6. **Review and preview.** The reel on a timeline with gapless preview of the edit (the strata-react edit-list player), swap or trim a scene, ask for "another version". Where a trim falls inside a shot is a subjective cut: strata-models' role `cut-points` (later) scores it on whether it breaks a spoken phrase or interrupts a movement, and people judge it blind A/B; until then trims snap to phrase ends from the transcript.
7. **Restore, render and export.** Restoration comes here, late, from the raw source (Jonathan, 2026-11-23): ask strata-models' `video-enhance` for the spans the reel uses (or the whole tape, if that proves simpler once the role's cost on the GPU is known) and store each as a derived asset that records the role, model, settings hash and parameters that made it. Everything before this step reads the raw tape. Then the ffmpeg render of the reel from the restored assets, with a render manifest (revision, inputs, versions, checksum); OpenTimelineIO export.

Done when: a real tape goes through all seven steps on the GPU machine, the reel is watchable without manual fixes beyond overrides, and the time model's round-trip tests pass (source to timeline to source, frame-exact on 25 fps interlaced PAL).

## Later (order to be agreed)

- **R2: people.** Who is in each scene, from strata-models' face recognition (identities live there, shared with the other apps, not here); "every scene with Grandma" as a pin. Recognition is opt-in per project, with a way to delete a person's face data and to withdraw consent, which invalidates the results that used it (spec S2, §6, risk register).
- **R3: race footage.** 360° clips as a second kind of source, written fresh from strata-360's lessons (no code ported); heavy 360 processing such as stitching lives outside this repo, as restoration does. strata-360 retires once reels does what it does.
- **R4: the agent loop.** MCP server over the same API as the UI (spec §7): search, inspect, propose, validate, commit with compare-and-swap on the revision.
- Speaker diarization, sound events, music and voice-over (strata-360 §17), reframing to 9:16 (spec S7), as strata-models gains the roles.
