# Progress log

## 2026-11-24: the first real tape

- Asked for: run the whole tape pipeline on a real VHS family tape on the GPU machine, evaluate its shot and scene boundaries, check the transcript, descriptions and faces, and tune.
- The run: a 21-minute PAL tape (interlaced, torn restarts, the signal lost at the end), every step done, no failure, 16 min 52 s while the machine was busy with other work: shots 151 s, faces 5 s, transcript 23 s, 47 descriptions at about 6 s each; most of the rest is ffmpeg.
- **Shots.** 50 cuts marked by eye. strata-models' 0.3 found 46 and made 7 false ones, all in the lost signal at the end; its 0.2 with lost signal found as black finds 49 and makes none (strata-models, branch `shots-vhs`, eval `shots-vhs`). The one left is six frames after another. Head-switching noise, chroma flashes and fast pans made no false cut.
- **Scenes.** 47 scenes, nearly one a shot, 7 of them black flashes. 36 boundaries marked as a new scene or not (11 left out as arguable): 23 right at 0.82. A shot is now compared by all its keyframes and `similar` is 0.67, `spread` 0.05: 31 right and 23 scenes. What is still wrong: portraits of people in one room are split (little but the furniture is alike), and two rooms with the same people are joined. Better needs more than a picture: the speech, or the describer asked whether two neighbours are one event.
- **Keyframes.** One a shot was the weakest part: 40 of the 47 scenes were a single shot, so a scene of up to 134 s was described from one frame, some of them wrongly, and counted from one (1 face where there were 3). Now one about every six seconds, and six to the describer.
- **Descriptions.** Right about what is in the frames, from the local model, in plain words; but the preset's own talk of tape faults made it call all 40 scenes noisy with tracking lines and 32 poor, and it counted people in photographs. Preset `scene-describe-v2` (strata-models) fixes both. Quality is "fair" nearly everywhere with v2: an honest word for VHS, not yet something step 5 can rank on.
- **Transcript.** English, 173 segments; most read as sense and fit the pictures (not checked by ear), though the talk is far from the camera and names come out several ways. Whisper's own two signals marked none doubtful, while one question came five times running and one word seventeen: text that repeats itself, words too fast to say and words of little probability are now doubtful too. Singing is left out by the voice filter.
- **Faces.** 140 on 159 sample frames at 360 lines and 138 at 540, so 360 is enough. Looked at on a sheet, none is on something that is not a face; a few are faces in photographs on a wall, and faces turned away or bent over are missed.
- **derive.frames** failed past 100 keyframes on this machine's ffmpeg 9: fixed in strata-python #77. A tape of over 100 shots would have stopped there.
- The integration tests asked the real strata-models on the machine that runs it; they no longer can.
- Not done: other tapes (this is the one digitised so far), and recordings with blue-screen or snow between them, which are only generated in strata-models' tests.

## 2026-11-24: the tape's analysis as one pipeline

- Asked for: update to the latest strata-python and strata-react and make full use of what they gained. strata-python 0.2.17 added `workflow` (a `Pipeline` of named, versioned stages over one subject, its records in an append-only table, one queue job per subject, shared routes) and `request_key`; strata-react added `useWorkflowRun` and `WorkflowSteps` to draw it. Jonathan chose to move the job chain onto the pipeline now rather than later.
- Before: eleven kinds of job, each queueing the next (the hash queued the assets, the shots and the speech; the shots the keyframes; they the scenes; the scenes the faces and descriptions), and the describe job had to wait for the transcript by queueing itself again, with a race fixed by not deduping. After: one `tape` pipeline (`jobs.TAPE`), one job per tape that runs each stage not done for the tape's inputs. A stage names what it needs, so describe simply needs the transcript and the race is gone; a failed stage blocks only what needs it, the job fails, and the queue's retry starts at the failed stage.
- The results table stays the cache (spec §6): a stage's record holds the id and key of the result it stood on, so the stage after it runs again only when that is another result. A derived stage's version is its recipe's key, so a changed recipe makes it again.
- One job per tape holds the `disk` resource for the whole run, so two tapes are never read from one drive at once, but a tape's GPU stages no longer overlap its ffmpeg ones. Fine for a home queue; worth looking at when a tape's analysis is long enough that the order matters.
- The tape page gained a Steps tab (`WorkflowSteps`: each step's state in words, a failure in the server's words, Try again) with the job's progress over it while it runs, and the finding names a failed step. The jobs page names each job "Analyse <tape>", its progress the step it is on.
- Not done: gates (a person approving a step) have no use yet; they come with corrections.

## 2026-11-24: the scene browser (R1 step 4, first half)

- Asked for: while the GPU machine is offline, a web view of a tape's scenes (thumbnail strips, transcript, descriptions, face counts) and the jobs screen deferred from step 1b, with the reusable parts in strata-react.
- One read for the page (`GET /api/sources/<id>/scenes`, `browse.py`) puts the results together and says, per step, what exists: a step that has not run is `null`, never an empty list, so a tape part way through its jobs reads as "not described yet", not "nothing said". Before there are scenes, `waiting` names the job they wait for. The transcript and the waveform are reads of their own.
- Pictures of a scene are the 10 s thumbnails inside it; a scene shorter than the step gets its shots' keyframes. Files are served by the names in their result's payload only, so a name from the request never becomes a path.
- The waveform is reduced on the server (each slice keeps its lowest and highest), since a three-hour tape at 50 slices a second is half a million pairs. It is drawn normalised: camcorder sound peaks near a tenth of full scale and was a flat line. The scaling is strata-react's (`Waveform normalise`, a 99.5th percentile with a ceiling of 16× so hiss stays small) and the plot says by how much.
- strata-react gained `FilmStrip` (a row of frames that fits its width, each with its time, a press to play from it, the frame being watched marked) and `Waveform normalise`, both in strata-react's PR for this thread; this app takes them from the release after it.
- The jobs page is strata-react's `JobsTable` over strata-python's `add_jobs` routes, which reels now mounts; each job is named by what it does to which tape ("Find the shots in christmas-1996.mkv"). It refreshes every 3 s while anything is waiting or running.
- Not done: corrections (a boundary moved, a description rewritten) stored as overrides, the rest of step 4; a look at a real tape's page on the GPU machine.

## 2026-11-24: speech, faces and descriptions (R1 step 3)

- Asked for: carry on with the plan after step 2: transcription, descriptions and who is in each scene, and the real-tape run if the GPU machine can be reached. It could not (offline all day), so everything here is checked against strata-models stood in for.
- The prompt that describes a scene lives in strata-models, not here: a new role `scene-describe` with preset `scene-describe-v1` and a strict JSON schema (strata-models #120), served by Qwen3.5-9B like `vision-language`, with Claude Haiku as the other candidate. It keeps what is seen (`observed`) apart from guesses (`inferred`, each with its evidence: spec S4), counts people and children, never names anyone, and lists the picture's faults (dark, blurry, shaky, noisy, tracking lines, colour cast, overexposed), which step 5 can score on. A better prompt is a new preset there and shows here as a new parameter, so the old descriptions are superseded rather than mixed in.
- Speech: one transcript per tape, not per scene, so Whisper hears whole sentences and a regrouping of scenes does not ask again. Its voice activity filter is on; segments Whisper itself doubts (its own `no_speech_threshold` 0.6 and `logprob_threshold` -1.0) are kept and marked, and a scene is told only the rest. A tape's hiss is where Whisper invents "Thank you." and the like.
- Descriptions: one result per scene, spanning it, so a long tape is described scene by scene and a job that stops keeps what it finished. Each sees up to three keyframes spread through the scene, its length, shot count and up to 1,500 characters of its speech. On a tape with sound the describe job waits for the transcript.
- Faces: boxes and detection scores on every keyframe; no embeddings, so nobody can be recognised before R2 asks who may be. A scene's count is the most faces on one of its keyframes, a floor: a keyframe is one frame of a shot.
- Not done: the run on a real tape; an eval of the describer on tape scenes (the `scene-describe` role has none yet); an API (with step 4's chapters screen, the first reader).

## 2026-11-24: shots and scenes (R1 step 2)

- Asked for: start the next step of the plan, turning a tape into scenes.
- Shots come from strata-models' `shot-boundaries` (ffmpeg's scene score after yadif, the role's baseline since strata-models #102). Its answer gives each cut's frame, so the frame is what is stored and every time is frame over the stream's rate, exact. Black stretches are snapped to frames, and a shot that is 90 % black is marked as a gap.
- Grouping: what says two neighbouring shots on a home tape belong to one event is that they look alike (the same room after the camcorder was restarted) and that there is no black between them. Each shot gets one keyframe, its middle frame, made with strata-python's new `derive.frames` (strata-python #69), because a VHS shot is often shorter than the thumbnails' 10 s step. strata-models' `image-embed` (CLIP ViT-B/32) gives each keyframe a vector, and neighbouring shots are compared by cosine. The confidence that a cut starts a scene is a logistic around a cosine of 0.82, 0.04 wide; black at or between the shots raises it to at least 0.9. These numbers are guesses, not measurements.
- Spec S5 asks for a hierarchy of boundaries with a confidence and a reason, not a flat partition. So every cut is kept with its level (`scene` at a confidence of one half or more, else `shot`), its confidence, the similarity and its reasons in words ("pictures alike (0.91)", "black at the cut"). The grouping is a pure function stored as a result (role `reels-scenes`, version 1) keyed on the shots and embeddings it read.
- Checked: the unit suite with strata-models stood in for over real HTTP, and an integration test that cuts real keyframes from a real file (red, dark red, black, blue) and compares their real average colours in place of CLIP. Red and dark red come out as one scene, black as the gap, and blue as another.
- Not done: no run on a real tape yet. The plan's other prerequisites also wait for the GPU machine: `shots-spliced` on the real race footage, and a check of the shot detector on tape faults. The thresholds need a tape whose scenes a person has marked, and step 4's chapters screen makes such corrections. No API for scenes yet; it comes with the chapters screen, its first reader. A shot under a second between two shots of one place (a glitch at a record-pause) ends up as a scene of its own; whether that happens on real tapes is to be seen.

## 2026-11-23: the records (R1 step 1b)

- Built the tables every later step writes into (overview §6): `results` (append-only; a re-run with another model or settings supersedes; cache key over inputs, role, model, settings hash and parameters), `overrides` (kept apart from results; a change is a new row, a withdrawal a mark), and strata-python's `jobs` table with this app's job kinds. The first kind is `hash`: each new source's full SHA-256, read in a job process while the server is up, or by `./app work`.
- Provenance comes from strata-models as it ran: #103 (PR #104) records each job's model, options and settings hash when it starts and returns them from `run_with_provenance`; `results.Provenance.of` takes that answer as it comes.
- A result about a derived asset still names its source (`source_id`); what it read exactly is in `inputs`, by hash. Overrides point at a result, scene or clip by kind and id (scenes and clips arrive in steps 2 and 5), not by a foreign key.
- Not built yet: derived assets, revisions and renders (with the steps that make them); an API or screen for jobs (the System page is the natural place, with step 2's first long job). Hashing reports no progress: `files.sha256_hex` has no hook for it, and a tape of a few GB reads in seconds to a minute.

## 2026-11-09: web app rebuilt on strata-react

- Asked for: make the template use strata-react fully, as an example and starter for new apps, with Storybook and visual testing.
- The web app is now four screens built only from strata-react (`web/src/screens/`), each opening with its one question and the patterns it shows: Greetings (`useLoad` → `DataView` → `DataTable` with sort in the URL, `RowAction` + `useUndoable` + `UndoBar`, `useSelection` + `BulkBar`, `Field` + `AsyncButton`, `MetricStrip`), Log (`useInfiniteLoad` over a cursor API → `InfiniteList`), System (polled read with `StaleNotice`, `Meter` with thresholds, `CompositionBar`, `Status`, `LinkCard`) and Settings (`Tabs` in the URL, a form started from the server's values, `ColorSchemeChoice`). The hand-written `ui.tsx`, hash router and `useLoad` are gone.
- Routing moved from `#/` hashes to real paths with React Router. To keep one build working behind a reverse proxy at a prefix, the server now answers every non-API path with the app and adds a relative `<base href>` that leads back to its root (`with_base` in `server/app.py`); `api.ts` `BASE` reads it and React Router takes it as `basename`. Checked by hand through a proxy at `/pre/`: deep links, a nested unknown path, sending and removing all worked with no failed request.
- API: `GET /api/system` (`system.py`), `GET /api/messages/page?after=&limit=` (a cursor, not an offset) and `DELETE /api/messages/{id}`. Ids were `len(items) + 1`, which reuses an id once a greeting is removed; they now come from `messages.seq` and are never reused (the web app hides a removed row by id until the removal commits, so a reused id would hide a new greeting).
- Theme: generated by ux-research `palette.mjs` from the subject (postmark ink and stamp red; 42 checks pass); status roles reuse strata-react's fixture values after measuring them on this palette's grounds (every ink at least 6.0:1). Faces are bundled (`@fontsource-variable`), which the visual suite requires.
- Tests: the jsdom suite renders the app's own route tree with `renderRoutes` from `strata-react/testing`, against MSW handlers that now live in `web/src/mocks/` and also serve Storybook and the visual suite. 40 web tests, 100% coverage (floors raised to 100); Python unit coverage 100%.
- Visual: `strata-visual` screenshots every story and the production build's routes (wide and phone) light and dark: 76 baselines, written here in the pinned Chromium (Playwright 1.63.0) and re-run clean. A long, auto-paging log is not screenshotted: which rows are in view depends on when pages land (CI and this machine disagreed on the first run); its paging is covered in jsdom. New CI job `visual` in `unit.yml`.
- Gaps in strata-react, raised as issues rather than worked around: `HttpError` drops the response body, so the server's 400 reason (`detail`) cannot be shown and `api.ts` repeats the server's limits client-side (jburnhams/strata-react#42); `CompositionBar` prints 0% for a part that is small but not zero (jburnhams/strata-react#43).
- One filled button at a time: a review saw the bulk "Remove 2" and "Send greeting" both filled once rows were chosen. The ux-research docs said "one filled per view" and "BulkBar: one filled" without saying which wins; the decision (recorded in ux-research `ui-library/patterns.md` §2 and strata-react's `CLAUDE.md`) is that while rows are chosen the bulk action is the filled one and the screen's primary steps down to outline. `GreetingForm` takes `primary`; the bulk-bar test checks exactly one filled button before, during and after a selection; the four `greetings--selected` baselines changed for it.
- Left out on purpose, as this sample has no use for them: keyboard queues (`useActiveItem`, `useHotkeys`), `SelectableGrid`/`Thumbnail`, `LineChart`, `Disclosure`/row expansion, `Done`.
